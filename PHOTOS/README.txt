Shoot one project at a time with the regular 1× lens, tripod, locked exposure and locked white balance. Use diffuse lighting and photograph a neutral reference for the session. Use matte blue for ordinary stock, a light backdrop for blue or navy boxes, and a white card above and in front of glossy panels. Leave space around corners, lids and inserts. The renderer supplies approximately 80% square framing.

Run through site/tools/WORKHORSE.md. Inspect a delivered cutout and master at full size before continuing a large shoot.
